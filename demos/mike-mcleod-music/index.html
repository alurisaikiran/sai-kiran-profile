The background

Decades of live and studio experience in Atlanta.

Mike McLeod has spent over 20 years performing across the Atlanta area — clubs, corporate stages, private estates, and waterfront venues. He came up as a guitarist and songwriter, worked in recording studios, and developed his looping technique to bring a full band's worth of sound to solo bookings.

His sets move with the room. Arrival and cocktail hour get warm, acoustic-leaning sounds. As the night builds so does the energy — Mike layers in more instruments, lifts the tempo, and meets the crowd where they are. By the time the room is ready to dance, the set sounds like a full band is playing.

He performs across all genres — pop, rock, country, R&B, jazz, and everything in between — making him equally at home at a black-tie gala or a backyard celebration.
