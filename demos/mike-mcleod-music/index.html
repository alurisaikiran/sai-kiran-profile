The background

Decades of live experience in Atlanta.

Mike McLeod has performed live across the Southeast for decades, with 25 years in Atlanta. His current chapter is the most exciting yet — as a solo looping performer, Mike plays the most epic songs of our time, and sounds "like the record"... at one-third the cost of a comparable band!

He delivers authentic performances in many genres — Top40 / Pop, R&B, Classic Rock, 80's, even pretty dinner music. Mike plays for prestigious corporate / non-profit clients, weddings, private / neighborhood parties, frats, you name it. He's equally at home at a black-tie gala or a backyard celebration.

Mike's method of looping is unique, creating an experience you've never seen before. It's organic, fascinating to watch, at the perfect volume and in stereo.
